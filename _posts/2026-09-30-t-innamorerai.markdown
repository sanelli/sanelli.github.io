Today, after thirty or thirty-five years, I listened to [this album](https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/T'innamorerai) again. A blast from the past, in the best way. I think an older cousin on my mom's side copied the cassette for her. She was my mom's cousin more than mine, I think; I have never been good at who is who in our not-so-small extended family. What I do remember is listening to that tape in the car with my parents. I wonder what happened to it. To any of the cassettes, really.

I am not ten anymore, so the album hits differently. The title roughly means "You will fall in love," but this is not a record about love love love. It is a record about emotions. I will probably put it on repeat again.

**Favorite opener.** *Vaffanculo.* Literally "Fuck you." Masini spends the first song telling a lot of people from his past to get lost, especially the producers who did not believe in him because he had a "loser face." "Hai la faccia da perdente, mi dispiace non funzioni Masini." I still remember watching him sing it on TV, some show with Pippo Baudo presenting, likely *Domenica in*. Rage as track one. What a way to start.

**Favorite closer.** *Un piccolo Chopin.* "A little Chopin." He remembers being different: watching his peers play football from the window while he learned piano, because he wanted to be a musician. That dream obviously came true. I relate a lot to this last part, not necessarily for loving music, but because I was different in many ways growing up. Gay and nerd were not things people really understood or appreciated in the nineties.

**Favorite lyrics.** *Dio non c'è.* He proclaims that there is no God, and that faith is a gift from God, which is also the thing he says does not exist. I will probably write a different post about atheism, and why I am an atheist myself. For now: it is good to hear a song from the nineties this clear about it. No God. Just lonely human beings on this earth.

**Special mention.** *Cantano i ragazzi.* The kids sing. Dreams of growing up. Such a masterpiece.

**And yes, there is still love.** The title track is bittersweet. Masini wishes the person he loves will fall in love. In one stretch he even wishes she will fall for a bastard and a liar. He still hopes love for her, even if that love will not be him.

<aside class="album-facts">
  <p class="album-facts-label">Album information</p>
  <p><em>T'innamorerai</em> is Marco Masini's third studio album, released on 14 January 1993 by Dischi Ricordi and produced by Giancarlo Bigazzi. Nine tracks, pop, about forty-six minutes. "Vaffanculo" caused a fuss on TV and radio; the album still spent seven weeks at number two in Italy and sold over a million copies in Europe. A Spanish version, <em>Te enamorarás</em>, went gold in Spain.</p>
</aside>
